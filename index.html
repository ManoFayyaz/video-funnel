The video editing skills you will gain

Five skills that take you from raw clips to finished, professional videos. Scroll to stack them up.

Cut like a pro editor

Learn to turn raw footage into a smooth, gripping video that holds attention from the first second.

  • Organise footage and build a clean timeline
  • Pace, trim and cut to the rhythm of the story
  • Use J-cuts, L-cuts and transitions that feel natural

Add motion graphics and titles

Make your videos look polished with animated text, logos and graphics that move with purpose.

  • Create animated titles and lower thirds
  • Use keyframes to make anything move smoothly
  • Build reusable templates to save hours

Master colour and look

Give every video a consistent, cinematic look instead of flat, mismatched footage.

  • Fix exposure and white balance quickly
  • Colour grade for a mood: warm, moody or clean
  • Match shots from different cameras and phones

Get clean, professional sound

Great video falls apart with bad audio. Learn to make voices clear and music sit right.

  • Remove background noise and echo
  • Balance voice, music and effects
  • Add sound design that makes cuts feel sharper

Export and publish for every platform

Finish strong with the right settings for each place your video will be seen.

  • Best export settings for YouTube, Reels and TikTok
  • Captions, thumbnails and aspect ratios
  • Build a repeatable workflow from raw clip to upload
